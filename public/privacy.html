Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Welcome to HostelX. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Student Application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide hostel management services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Details: Name, Student ID, and contact information provided by your hostel administration.
  • Facial Recognition Data: Used locally on your device for attendance verification. We do not store raw facial images on our servers; only encrypted mathematical representations (embeddings).
  • Location Data: Used to verify your presence within the hostel geofence during attendance. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app (e.g., leave requests, complaints).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Facilitate automated attendance marking.
  • Process leave and out-pass requests.
  • Manage and track student complaints.
  • Ensure the security and safety of the hostel premises.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS.

4. Third-Party Services

HostelX may use third-party services like Vercel for hosting and MongoDB for data storage. These services have their own privacy policies regarding data handling.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. Please contact your hostel administrator or our support team for such requests.
